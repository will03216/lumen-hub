# F8 · Interactive AR Effects

- **Creative essence**: branded AR stickers / filters / green screens / interactive effects for users to create UGC with, driving viral spread
- **P/B positioning**: pure B (engagement / UGC)
- **Category direction**: brands that drive UGC (try-on-able, playable)
- **Brand references**: Ray-Ban, Disney, Nestlé

## Core scenario

**Channel family: Social Ads (engagement / UGC).**
This is not an ad "played at" users, but something that lets users **create with branded AR effects inside the camera interface** (virtual try-on, branded filters, interactive mini-games), so they spontaneously produce huge volumes of UGC that spreads virally. The core is that the effect itself has to be fun, easy to pick up, and imitable, and it is usually amplified alongside an official hashtag challenge. The plans here are for "demo films" that show how the effect works and drive participation.
