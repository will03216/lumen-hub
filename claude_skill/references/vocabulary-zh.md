# Vocabulary Zh

Use this file when the user wants prompt-ready Chinese film language.

## Shot sizes

- 大远景: extreme wide shot
- 远景: wide shot
- 全景: full shot
- 中景: medium shot
- 中近景: medium close-up
- 近景: close-up
- 特写: extreme close-up
- 过肩镜头: over-the-shoulder

## Camera movement

- 固定机位: locked-off
- 推镜头: dolly push-in
- 拉镜头: dolly pull-out
- 摇镜头: pan
- 升降摇: tilt
- 环绕: orbit
- 跟拍: tracking follow
- 手持镜头: handheld
- 稳定器跟拍: steadicam
- 一镜到底: one-take
- 甩镜: whip pan
- 焦点转换: rack focus

## Lighting and atmosphere

- 主光: key light
- 辅光 / 补光: fill light
- 轮廓光: rim light
- 逆光: backlight
- 柔光: soft light
- 硬光: hard light
- 黄金时刻: golden hour
- 冷蓝调: cool blue
- 暖琥珀色: warm amber
- 薄雾: thin fog
- 浮尘微粒: dust motes
- 呼气白雾: breath mist

## Style and texture

- 变形宽银幕镜头: anamorphic lens
- 球面镜头: spherical lens
- 胶片颗粒: film grain
- 低饱和色调: muted palette
- 去饱和: desaturated
- 高对比: high contrast
- 赛璐璐渲染: cel-shaded
- 水彩渲染: watercolor wash
- 照片级写实: photorealistic

## Motion and timing

- 慢动作 / 升格: slow motion
- 正常速度: real-time
- 延时摄影: time-lapse
- 卡点: beat-sync
- 缓入: ease in
- 缓出: ease out
- 匹配剪辑: match cut

## 微表情与表演（prompt-ready）

配合 [`performance-direction.md`](performance-direction.md) 使用——写行为，不写情绪形容词。

- 下颌收紧: jaw tightens
- 嘴唇抿成一线: lips press into a thin line
- 嘴角压抑地上扬: corner of mouth pulls up, suppressed
- 下唇轻颤一下: lower lip trembles once
- 喉结滚动: a visible swallow
- 鼻翼微张: nostrils flare once
- 眨眼频率加快: blink rate quickens
- 眼神躲闪 / 瞟向门口: gaze darts to the door
- 目光垂落: gaze drops
- 眼眶泛红但不落泪: eyes well up without spilling
- 眉头紧锁: brows pull together
- 单边挑眉: single brow lifts
- 倒吸一口气: sharp inhale
- 屏住呼吸: holds breath
- 颤抖地呼气: shaky exhale
- 指节攥到发白: knuckles whiten on the grip
- 手悬在半空迟疑: reaching hand hesitates mid-air
- 缓缓别过头: slow turn away

## 多镜头标签

- 镜头1: / 镜头2: / 镜头3: — 等价于 `Shot 1:` 切镜标签，作为多镜头骨架
- 一镜到底，无剪辑: single continuous take, no cuts
- 在第5秒……: at 5 seconds…（仅作镜头内部节奏提示，不替代镜头标签）

## 写作工艺词汇 (prompt-prose-craft 中文对照)

### 动词物理（替代通用动词）
- 拖步: dragging steps (weight, fatigue)
- 踉跄: stumble (loss of control)
- 甩步: sharp stride (urgency)
- 猛推: shove open (force)
- 攥紧: clench (tension in hands)
- 颤抖: tremble (micro, subtle)
- 抽搐: twitch (involuntary micro)

### 时间标记词
- 起初…随后…最终: at first… then… finally
- 缓缓: slowly/gradually (adjacent to verb)
- 骤然: suddenly
- 始终: throughout the shot
- 片刻: for a moment / briefly

### 感官压缩短语 (material + condition + light)
- `湿沥青反射霓虹` (wet asphalt reflecting neon)
- `锈蚀栏杆上凝着晨露` (rusted railing with dawn dew)
- `斑驳墙面斜阳切入` (mottled wall, angled sunlight)
- `蒸汽在冷白荧光中升腾` (steam rising in cold fluorescent)
- `尘埃悬停在窗光柱中` (dust motes in a window shaft)

### 材质抗塑料词
- 磨砂: matte / frosted
- 做旧: aged / distressed
- 氧化: oxidized
- 斑驳: mottled / patchy
- 褪色: faded
- 粗糙纹理: rough texture / open grain
- 哑光: low-gloss
- 磨损: worn / scuffed

### 对比轴
- 冷暖对比: warm vs cool (`烛光暖黄面部，窗外冷蓝城市光`)
- 明暗对比: light vs shadow (`单束光照亮主体，其余深影`)
- 虚实对比: sharp vs soft (`面部锐利，背景化为散景`)
- 动静对比: still vs moving (`静帧中唯窗帘微动`)
