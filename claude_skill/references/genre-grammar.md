# Genre Grammar — the codified visual look of each genre

[`narrative-design.md`](narrative-design.md) covers genre *structure* (the
story shape). This file covers genre *grammar* — the lighting, camera, color,
and staging conventions an audience reads as "this is a horror film / a noir /
a romance" before a word is spoken. Load the matching profile and let it set
defaults for camera, light, color, and production design.

Use a genre profile to **choose priorities**, never to add empty style words.
Every shot still needs a subject, one beat, motivated light, and a constraint.

---

## Horror

| Lever | Convention |
|-------|-----------|
| Light | low-key, high contrast, deep blacks swallowing detail; a single bare bulb, TV flicker, flashlight as the only source |
| Composition | large dark negative space at the edges where "something could be"; obstructed views (door frames, curtains, foreground silhouettes) |
| Camera | slow inexorable push-in like a predator approaching; subtle dolly creep; occasionally off-level (canted) for unease; sometimes reveal *nothing* |
| Color | desaturated with sickly green or yellow contamination; blood red withheld, then shocking |
| Sound | low room tone, isolated foley, sudden silence before the hit |

> Low-key single-bulb light, deep black negative space frame-left, slow
> push-in toward the closed door, desaturated sickly-green cast, room tone with
> one floor creak. Nothing emerges. Locked-off final hold.

## Film noir / neo-noir

| Lever | Convention |
|-------|-----------|
| Light | hard directional key, sharp shadows, venetian-blind slats across faces and walls |
| Composition | low angles looking up past foreground elements (railings, lampposts); reflections in mirrors and wet glass; canted frames for moral imbalance |
| Camera | deep focus keeping the oppressive city ever-present |
| Color | B&W silver-halide contrast, or neo-noir's limited cyan / magenta / sodium-yellow |
| World | neon signs, wet asphalt, cigarette smoke catching the beam |

> Hard key from a window, venetian-blind shadows striping his face, low angle
> past a stair rail, wet-asphalt neon reflections, limited cyan-and-sodium
> palette, smoke drifting through the light.

## Thriller

| Lever | Convention |
|-------|-----------|
| Camera | starts steady/objective with clean geography, escalates to handheld + tighter lenses as paranoia rises; long-lens shots through windows, blinds, reflections (surveillance feel) |
| Composition | telephoto compression for "someone is watching" |
| Color | cool neutral baseline with sudden intrusions of emergency red/blue |
| Pacing | fragmentation increases with stakes (see pacing curve in [`continuity-and-editing.md`](continuity-and-editing.md)) |

## Romance

| Lever | Convention |
|-------|-----------|
| Light | soft wrapping key, bright eyes, gentle fill, subtle backlight halo; no harsh shadows |
| Composition | faces, proximity, hands; shallow depth of field, blurred backgrounds |
| Camera | slow graceful dolly/slider that breathes with the characters; an enveloping orbit at the peak |
| Color | warm golden tones, soft pastels, late-afternoon sun; gentle haze and bokeh |

> Soft wrapping key with a backlight halo, shallow focus on their hands meeting,
> slow dolly-in, warm golden late-afternoon palette, gentle lens haze.

## Documentary / vérité

| Lever | Convention |
|-------|-----------|
| Camera | handheld, reactive, slightly imperfect; reframes mid-shot; occasional snap zoom |
| Light | available light only, mixed color temperatures, imperfect exposure |
| Framing | off-center, variable headroom — moment prioritized over composition |
| Sound | location ambience, no scored music under the moment |

## Commercial / high-end advertising

| Lever | Convention |
|-------|-----------|
| Light | spotless studio-clean, controlled specular highlights on the product, smooth minimal background |
| Camera | impossibly smooth motion-control arcs, parallax, macro for tactile detail; often slow motion |
| Color | brand-driven; background hues support, never compete with, the product color |
| Rule | preserve product identity — move light/mist/camera around it, don't transform it (see [`assets.md`](assets.md) for product refs) |

## Action

One objective, one obstacle, one camera move per shot, explicit character tags.
Specify force, timing, consequence: who ducks, who lands, what breaks, what
stays intact. If hands/faces/props matter, reduce action density or split into
clips. Beat-count drives duration ([`duration-calculus.md`](duration-calculus.md)).

## Animation / stylized

Direct medium, line quality, palette, deformation, timing, texture: `soft
squash-and-stretch`, `stepped limited animation`, `cut-paper parallax`,
`clay-like stop motion`, `clean 3D toy realism`. Describe original character
shapes — never name a studio, franchise, or artist ([`safety.md`](safety.md)).

---

## Mixing & tone

Genres can blend, but pick a **dominant** grammar and let the second flavor it
(`romance lit and staged, with one noir shadow when he lies`). Manage tone as
its own arc — e.g. start lightly comic with bright playful compositions, then
flatten the palette and straighten the framing as the humor drains out.
